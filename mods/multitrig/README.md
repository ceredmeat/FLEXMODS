# MULTITRIG 0.5 (Digitakt MKI, OS 1.54)

Edit many trigs' locks at once, in **GRID RECORDING**.

| Gesture | What it selects |
|---|---|
| Hold a trig, **tap PAGE** | every trig on the page you see |
| Hold a trig, **tap TRK** | every trig on the track |

Then turn any knob: the trig you hold changes as on stock, and all the selected trigs with it. Let
go of the trig and the selection clears. The other selected trigs don't light up as held: only the
one under your finger does.

## SETTINGS

| Row | |
|---|---|
| MULTI EDIT | **REL**: each trig keeps its own value and they move together, as with several held trigs on stock: transpose a page or a track. **ABS**: every selected trig takes the value of the trig you hold (with PAGE on its own, the page's first trig), and they move together |

It is saved.

## Notes

- While a trig is held, TRK and PAGE belong to MULTITRIG: nothing else in the Digitakt sees them.
- Only steps with a trig are selected, so no new trigs appear.
- Tap TRK rather than hold it: TRK held with a knob is the stock CONTROL ALL.
